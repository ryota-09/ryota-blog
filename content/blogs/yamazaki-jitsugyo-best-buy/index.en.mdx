## The Ranking: Yamazaki Products Worth Buying

### #1: Range Hood Seasoning Rack (Plate)

This is my number one. **Installation is just hanging it on the edge of the range hood, yet the prime real estate around the stove turns into seasoning storage.**

![Range hood seasoning rack hooked on the edge of the range hood](./images/chomiryo-rack.jpg)

Our kitchen never had a good spot for seasonings, and for a while I kept them sitting right next to the stove. They get covered in oil that way..

Since installing this rack, that "turn around and hunt for the seasoning I need right now" move has disappeared from my cooking. Everything I want is right in front of me.

I actually ranked it 9th across the whole house in my [2026 first-half best-buy roundup](/en/blogs/zakki/best-buy-2026-first-half), but after three more months of daily use, it's the undisputed #1 for the kitchen.

It costs around 3,000 yen. No tools needed — you just hook it on and you're done.

A rack you simply place over the gap behind the stove.

## The One That Let Me Down

I've done nothing but praise so far, so let me be honest about the disappointment: the smart-series magnetic folding door stopper I bought in July 2026.

Long story short: **on our entrance door, it barely works as a door stopper.**

When you try to stop the door, the stopper slides out of place. It does hold if you fiddle with the positioning, but needing fine adjustments defeats the whole point of a "flip down and stop" door stopper..

The magnet itself is strong and it stays out of the way when mounted, so it's still on our door. But if you ask "was it worth buying?" — for us, no 😇

Compatibility likely depends on your door's material and shape, so I'd check the bottom profile of your entrance door before buying.

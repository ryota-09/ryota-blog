## Has the women's 0 yen sign-up fee campaign ended?

Short answer: **it disappeared from the official price table sometime in October**. But no "this campaign has ended" notice was posted anywhere.

Here's the timeline.

| Date | What could be confirmed | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 17 | A speech bubble reading "matchmaking support campaign — sign-up fee 0 yen for women" is added to the price table | Price-table image on the official site (last-modified date) |
| October 2 | The price table with the bubble is still up | Internet Archive |
| October 14 | A comparison site still says women pay 0 yen | Comparison-site article |
| October 21-22 | New sign-ups paused for a PHP vulnerability fix, then resumed the next day | Official X account and official site notice |
| November 1 | The price table is back to the regular version with no bubble (sign-up fee 17,800 yen, monthly fee 17,800 yen) | Official site |

I opened the top page myself on November 1 to check, and the price-table image itself had been swapped from the campaign version to the regular one without the bubble. The smartphone view showed the same thing.

All I can say about the timing is that it ended somewhere between mid-October and the end of the month... Whether it has anything to do with the October 21 maintenance isn't something you can tell from the outside. The official X account's October posts were only about the maintenance and the reopening of sign-ups — not a word about the campaign.

The August summer campaign at least had a stated deadline ("until August 30"), but this women's campaign never had an end date in the first place. When it ended, there was no notice either — the price table just quietly went back to normal. It's safer to assume Hitooshi's discounts move without warning.

So if you're a woman and you've seen "0 yen sign-up fee right now" on some other site, ask through the official LINE account whether it still applies before you apply. Even if it has ended, the invitation code below still gets you 5,000 yen off.

## A year of past campaigns

I went back through past versions of the official price table on the Internet Archive and lined them up with posts from the official X account. The dates mean "the offer was confirmed as listed at that time," not the actual campaign periods (where a deadline was announced, it's in parentheses).

| December 2025 | "One month completely free": sign-up fee 0 yen + first month's fee 0 yen (until December 21) | Kansai and Tokai areas |

The Internet Archive had no copy of the price-table image from June, so I confirmed the June women's campaign from the official announcement image reposted on a comparison site. My previous update missed both the Golden Week and June campaigns, so I've added them this time. Lined up like this, three patterns stand out.

- Big campaigns open to both men and women came three times a year — January, Golden Week and August — and each was a waived sign-up fee plus the second month's fee at half price
- Women-only campaigns came in February, June and September-October, filling the gaps between the big ones
- Area-limited offers keep popping up in Kansai and Tokai

Flip it around and the only stretch where nobody at all qualified for anything — regardless of region or gender — was roughly March-April. November is the first such gap since then.

That said, "somebody qualified" is doing a lot of work there. A man living in Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama or Chiba, for example, could only use the three big campaigns this past year: January, Golden Week and August.

## Should you wait for a New Year campaign?

Honestly, this is where it gets hard to call.

There's been a campaign right after New Year two years running. In 2026 the official X account announced the "2026 marriage-hunting start campaign" early in January, and everyone got the sign-up fee waived plus one month at half price (the second month's fee was halved). For January 2025 too, there's a user's blog post introducing a New Year discount campaign.

The big everyone-eligible campaign takes the 17,800 yen sign-up fee down to 0 yen and halves the second month's fee, which is another 8,900 yen off. That's 26,700 yen in total. Compared with the invitation code's 5,000 yen, the gap is 21,700 yen.

So if you look only at the money, "wait for the New Year if you're not in a hurry" is a reasonable call. For women, women-only campaigns have also come in February, June and September, so there are a few more possible windows to wait for.

But there's no announcement yet of anything for this coming New Year. If nothing comes, you'll have waited two months for nothing, and you'll have missed people you might have met in the meantime. Hitooshi introduces two people a month, so a two-month delay pushes back four chances to meet someone.

If it were me, I'd split it like this: if you feel like starting this month, start with the invitation code; if you're more "starting in the New Year is fine," then wait.

One more thing that weighs as much as the discount: who you'd actually be meeting. I've also written about [the age range and looks of the people currently on Hitooshi](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-members), so take a peek before you apply.

If the interview leaves you unconvinced, you can simply choose not to proceed to full membership. For how the service actually went for me, my [hands-on review article](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-review) has the details.

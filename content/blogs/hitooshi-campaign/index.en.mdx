## Past campaigns and the pattern

Hitooshi's campaigns run irregularly. Recently the timeline looked like this.

- January 2026: New Year matchmaking campaign — sign-up fee waived + 1st month half price (until January 25)
- August 2026: Summer matchmaking support campaign — sign-up fee waived + 2nd month half price (until August 30), with an Amazon gift card giveaway running on X at the same time

The pattern: big "sign-up fee waived" offers arrive at moments when people start looking, like New Year and summer.

So is waiting for the next window the right call? Honestly, it's a tough one... No next date has been announced, and starting a few months later pushes every encounter a few months later too. Personally, I'd rather start with the 5,000 yen discount and work in a focused burst than wait — the monthly fees from a longer stint dwarf the difference in discounts.

One more thing that weighs as much as the discount: who you'd actually be meeting. I've also written about [the age range and looks of the people currently on Hitooshi](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-members), so take a peek before you apply.

If the interview leaves you unconvinced, you can simply choose not to proceed to full membership. For how the service actually went for me, my [hands-on review article](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-review) has the details.

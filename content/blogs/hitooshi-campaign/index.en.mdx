## What the summer campaign includes, and the conditions

This is the big one, displayed on the [official site](https://hito-oshi.com/) top page.

- Sign-up fee: 17,800 yen → 0 yen
- 2nd month's membership fee: 17,800 yen → 8,900 yen

That adds up to **26,700 yen in savings**. For the regular pricing and what 3-6 months of membership costs in total, see [my article on Hitooshi's pricing, sign-up fee and monthly fee](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-price).

One thing to watch is the fine print. The official banner says:

> *This campaign applies when you register your basic information and complete the booking of your first interview within the period.
>
> (From the campaign banner on the [official Hitooshi site](https://hito-oshi.com/))

In other words, registering alone isn't enough — you also need to book the first interview. I covered what the interview asks in [my article on the first interview questions and flow](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-interview).

Now, you might wonder: "what if I can book in time, but the interview date itself falls after August 30?" According to the official announcement, the campaign still applies as long as registration and booking are completed within the period, even if the interview takes place after August 30.

The 17,800 yen sign-up fee is the part everyone hesitates over right before applying... Having it disappear entirely is honestly a big deal.

## Past campaigns and the pattern

Hitooshi's campaigns run irregularly. Recently the timeline looked like this.

- January 2026: New Year matchmaking campaign — sign-up fee waived + 1st month half price (until January 25)
- August 2026: Summer matchmaking support campaign — sign-up fee waived + 2nd month half price (until August 30)

The pattern: big "sign-up fee waived" offers arrive at moments when people start looking, like New Year and summer.

Which also means that once this window closes, nobody knows when the next one comes... If you're on the fence, getting as far as booking the interview during the period is the rational move.

If the interview leaves you unconvinced, you can simply choose not to proceed to full membership. For how the service actually went for me, my [hands-on review article](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-review) has the details.

I work as an engineer in Tokyo. I'm also a former Hitooshi user — I used the matchmaking service for about 5-6 months before graduating from it.

This time I'm sorting out **the discounts you can use to join Hitooshi as of October 2026**, checked against the official site and the members-only pages!

The short answer: right now the discounts split by gender. Women get the sign-up fee waived entirely through the "matchmaking support campaign." There's no campaign for men at the moment — the only discount available to them is 5,000 yen off the sign-up fee with an invitation code.

## Discounts you can use right now (October 2026)

As of October 6, 2026, the official site and the members' page list these three.

| Perk | What you get | Who qualifies | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Matchmaking support campaign | Sign-up fee of 17,800 yen becomes 0 yen | Women | Not stated |
| Friend referral | 5,000 yen off the sign-up fee with an invitation code | Anyone with an invitation code (men and women) | Not stated |
| U29 campaign | Sign-up fee reduced by 5,000 yen to 12,800 yen | Women aged 29 or under | Not stated |

These don't stack. The fine print on the members' invitation page says that when another campaign is running, whichever has the highest discount rate is applied. So right now women of any age get the 0 yen sign-up fee, and for men the invitation code's 5,000 yen discount is the cheapest way in.

If you first want to know what Hitooshi even is, start with [my article explaining how matchmaking on Hitooshi works](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-konkatsu).

## Women: the sign-up fee is 0 yen

The speech bubble reading "matchmaking support campaign — sign-up fee 0 yen for women" appeared on the official price table in mid-September. So the next campaign started less than a month after the summer one ended in August.

That said, not much is known yet.

- No end date is given (nothing on the price table or on the official X account)
- The monthly fee is unchanged — only the sign-up fee is discounted
- The official X account hasn't posted since August 1, and there's been no announcement of this campaign

So I can't tell you "until when"... The summer campaign in August ended quietly with nothing more than a swap of the price-table image, so it's safer to assume this one could end without notice too.

To give you a feel for the amount: when you apply, you pay the sign-up fee plus the first month's fee. For women right now, that comes down to just the 17,800 yen monthly fee. Over the 3-month minimum contract, that's 17,800 yen cheaper than usual. I've simulated 3-, 6- and 12-month totals in [my article on Hitooshi's pricing, sign-up fee and monthly fee](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-price).

For women, entering an invitation code won't lower the sign-up fee any further. But it doesn't cost you anything either — the members' page says the referrer still receives their perk when a friend enters the code, even if another campaign is what gets applied. If this article helped you, I'd be happy if you entered the invitation code below when you apply ☺️

## U29 campaign (for women 29 and under)

The U29 campaign — the sign-up fee reduced by 5,000 yen to 12,800 yen for women aged 29 or under — is still listed on the official site.

That said, it has no role to play while the matchmaking support campaign is running. The bigger discount wins, so women 29 and under also get the 0 yen sign-up fee. U29 only matters again once the women's campaign ends.

For age requirements and which regions the service covers, see [my article on service areas and joining requirements](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-eligibility).

## A year of past campaigns

I went back through past versions of the official price table on the Internet Archive and lined them up with posts from the official X account. The dates mean "the offer was confirmed as listed at that time," not the actual campaign periods.

| When confirmed | What it was | Who qualified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 2025 | Sign-up fee 0 yen to celebrate the area launch | Kansai, Tokai and North Kanto areas |
| October 2025 | Monthly fee 8,900 yen for as long as you stay (until November 16) | Kansai and Tokai areas |
| December 2025 | First month's fee 0 yen | Kansai and Tokai areas |
| January 2026 | New Year campaign: sign-up fee waived + 1 month half price (until January 25) | Everyone |
| February 2026 | Matchmaking support campaign: first month's fee 0 yen | Women |
| July 2026 | First month's fee 0 yen | Kansai and Tokai areas |
| August 2026 | Summer campaign: sign-up fee 0 yen + 2nd month half price (until August 30) | Everyone |
| September 2026 onward | Matchmaking support campaign: sign-up fee 0 yen | Women |

Lined up like this, three patterns stand out.

- Big campaigns open to both men and women came only twice a year, in January and August
- Women-only campaigns came on their own, in February and September
- Area-limited offers keep popping up in Kansai and Tokai

From a man's point of view, there's no telling when the next campaign open to everyone will come. If it follows this January, it would be around New Year, but nothing has been announced.

So should you wait? Personally, I don't think so. Even the everyone-eligible campaign this January was worth 26,700 yen. Meanwhile, starting 3 months later pushes every encounter 3 months later too. If your search drags on, the monthly fee is 17,800 yen every month, so the difference in discounts gets eaten up fast.

One more thing that weighs as much as the discount: who you'd actually be meeting. I've also written about [the age range and looks of the people currently on Hitooshi](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-members), so take a peek before you apply.

If the interview leaves you unconvinced, you can simply choose not to proceed to full membership. For how the service actually went for me, my [hands-on review article](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-review) has the details.

## Wrapping up

Here's the summary of Hitooshi's campaign situation as of October 2026.

- Women get the sign-up fee waived through the matchmaking support campaign (no end date announced)
- There's no campaign for men; the invitation code's 5,000 yen discount is the only one
- Discounts don't stack — the biggest one is applied automatically
- No official coupons, point-site deals, or student discounts exist right now
- Big everyone-eligible campaigns came in January and August; women-only ones in February and September

Campaigns change without notice, so I'll update this article whenever the situation changes.

The short answer first. When I opened the official site on November 6, a "free sign-up fee campaign" had started for people living in Kansai and Tokai only. The 17,800 yen sign-up fee drops to 0 yen, and the deadline is Sunday, December 6.

If you live in Kanto, you're not covered, so the invitation code's 5,000 yen off the sign-up fee is still the cheapest way in.

And I owe you an apology. In my November 1 update I wrote that "there's no limited-time campaign right now," and this campaign started just a few days later. I'll go into exactly when it started below.

## Discounts you can use right now (November 2026)

As of November 6, 2026, the official site and the members' page list these three.

| Perk | What you get | Who qualifies | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kansai/Tokai-only free sign-up fee campaign | 17,800 yen sign-up fee drops to 0 yen | People living in the 10 Kansai and Tokai prefectures (men and women) | Until Sunday, December 6 |
| Friend referral | 5,000 yen off the sign-up fee with an invitation code | Anyone with an invitation code (men and women) | Not stated |
| U29 campaign | Sign-up fee reduced by 5,000 yen to 12,800 yen | Women aged 29 or under | Not stated (no longer shown on the official top page) |

Discounts don't stack. The fine print on the members' invitation page says that when another campaign is running, whichever has the highest discount rate is applied.

So the answer depends on where you live.

- If you live in Kansai or Tokai: the free sign-up fee campaign is the best deal (sign-up fee 0 yen)
- If you live in Kanto: the invitation code's 5,000 yen off is the best deal (sign-up fee 12,800 yen)

Women 29 and under living in Kanto end up with a 12,800 yen sign-up fee whether U29 or the invitation code applies.

## The Kansai/Tokai-only free sign-up fee campaign (until December 6)

Here's what the campaign on the official top page and price table says.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Free sign-up fee campaign (called the "matchmaking support campaign" in the price-table speech bubble) |
| Discount | Sign-up fee 17,800 yen → 0 yen |
| Who qualifies | People living in the Kansai area (Osaka, Kyoto, Hyogo, Shiga, Nara, Wakayama) or the Tokai area (Aichi, Gifu, Mie, Shizuoka) |
| Condition | Register your basic information and book your first interview |
| Deadline | Until Sunday, December 6 |
| Monthly fee | No discount (still 17,800 yen) |

There's no gender restriction. The August summer campaign also halved the second month's fee, but this one covers the sign-up fee only.

The thing to watch is that the condition is "first interview booked." It's not enough to just apply — you need to have booked the interview by December 6. For the summer campaign, the official announcement said the interview date itself could fall after the deadline, but I couldn't find anything like that this time. If you're applying right before the deadline, it's worth confirming through the official LINE account to be safe.

## In Kanto, the invitation code is the cheapest way in

If you live in Kanto (Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama, Chiba, Ibaraki, Tochigi, Gunma), this campaign doesn't apply to you. With a friend-referral invitation code, 5,000 yen comes off the 17,800 yen sign-up fee. It doesn't depend on whether a campaign is running — it's the standing discount that always works.

It's a 3-step process.

1. Apply via the official site or the official LINE account
2. When asked "How did you hear about Hitooshi?", choose "referred by an acquaintance"
3. Enter the code above (A2R7FE3K) in the invitation code field

After you apply, you move on to the first interview. I covered what it asks in [my article on the first interview questions and flow](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-interview), so if you're nervous, reading it first should take the edge off.

By the way, whether campaigns can be combined with each other is a completely separate question from using Hitooshi alongside other services like dating apps — the latter is not prohibited by the terms. Either way, for women 29 and under in Kanto, the sign-up fee is 12,800 yen with U29 or with the invitation code. There's not much reason to worry about whether U29 applies — enter the invitation code and you'll get the 5,000 yen off.

## A year of past campaigns

I went back through past versions of the official price table on the Internet Archive and lined them up with posts from the official X account. The dates mean "the offer was confirmed as listed at that time," not the actual campaign periods (where a deadline was announced, it's in parentheses).

| When confirmed | What it was | Who qualified |

| September 2025 | Sign-up fee 0 yen to celebrate the area launch | Kansai, Tokai and North Kanto areas |
| October 2025 | Monthly fee 8,900 yen for as long as you stay (until November 16) | Kansai and Tokai areas |
| December 2025 | "One month completely free": sign-up fee 0 yen + first month's fee 0 yen (until December 21) | Kansai and Tokai areas |

The Internet Archive had no copy of the price-table image from June, so I confirmed the June women's campaign from the official announcement image reposted on a comparison site.

Lined up like this, three patterns stand out.

- Big campaigns open to both men and women came three times a year — January, Golden Week and August — and each was a waived sign-up fee plus the second month's fee at half price
- Women-only campaigns came in February, June and September-October, filling the gaps between the big ones
- Offers aimed at Kansai and Tokai are the most frequent: five in the past year (September, October and December 2025; July and November 2026)

In other words, people living in Kansai or Tokai were eligible for something roughly once every two to three months. A man living in Tokyo, Kanagawa, Saitama or Chiba, on the other hand, could only use the three big campaigns this past year: January, Golden Week and August.

## Should you wait for a New Year campaign?

The answer depends on where you live.

If you live in Kansai or Tokai, I don't think there's much reason to wait. This campaign already takes the sign-up fee to 0 yen, so even if a big everyone-eligible campaign comes after New Year, the only extra you'd get is the second month at half price — 8,900 yen. Rather than wait two months for 8,900 yen that may never come, I'd get the interview booked by December 6.

If you live in Kanto, honestly, it's a harder call.

There's been a campaign right after New Year two years running. In 2026 the official X account announced the "2026 marriage-hunting start campaign" early in January, and everyone got the sign-up fee waived plus one month at half price (the second month's fee was halved). For January 2025 too, there's a user's blog post introducing a New Year discount campaign.

The big everyone-eligible campaign takes the 17,800 yen sign-up fee down to 0 yen and halves the second month's fee, which is another 8,900 yen off. That's 26,700 yen in total. Compared with the invitation code's 5,000 yen, the gap is 21,700 yen. If you look only at the money, "wait for the New Year if you're not in a hurry" is a reasonable call.

But there's no announcement yet of anything for this coming New Year. If nothing comes, you'll have waited two months for nothing, and you'll have missed people you might have met in the meantime. Hitooshi introduces two people a month, so a two-month delay pushes back four chances to meet someone.

If it were me, I'd split it like this: if you feel like starting this month, start with the invitation code; if you're more "starting in the New Year is fine," then wait.

One more thing that weighs as much as the discount: who you'd actually be meeting. I've also written about [the age range and looks of the people currently on Hitooshi](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-members), so take a peek before you apply.

If the interview leaves you unconvinced, you can simply choose not to proceed to full membership. For how the service actually went for me, my [hands-on review article](/en/blogs/zakki/hitooshi-review) has the details.

## Wrapping up

Here's the summary of Hitooshi's campaign situation as of November 2026.

- If you live in one of the 10 Kansai and Tokai prefectures, the sign-up fee is 0 yen until Sunday, December 6 (as long as you've booked your first interview)
- If you live in Kanto, the invitation code's 5,000 yen discount is the cheapest way in
- The women's 0 yen sign-up fee from September-October ended during October
- U29 is no longer shown on the official top page, but it's the same amount as the invitation code anyway
- No official coupons, point-site deals, or student discounts exist right now

Hitooshi's campaigns start and end without any announcement. Learning from this miss, I now watch the official price table more closely, so I'll update this article again whenever things change.
